# Oil Cost-Basis Savings Model — Realized PnL Credits

> **Hypothetical paper-model accounting view only.** This report does not represent verified brokerage executions, an account return, or a recommendation. Costs, commissions, financing, spread, and execution effects are not modeled.

## Method and scope

At the user's direction, the realized 10U Manual-Automatic target-trim PnL, separate realized 10U SHORT PnL, and paired 50U cash-out PnL are credited against the combined remaining open-long basis for that ticker. The legacy target-trim/short basis table below retains its narrower 60U tagged basket (the original 40U residual plus two manual replenishment lots); the separate cash-out is an additional credit reclassified against that basket. The four replenishments are all recorded on 2026-10-25: the $115.792 BRENT/$101.74 OIL entries use the user-recalled 17:20–17:25 MST window, modeled at a 17:22 midpoint, and the $114.80 BRENT/$101.00 OIL entries use approximately 20:00 MST. These are approximate ordering assumptions, not verified transaction times; the user's supplied replenishment prices are retained. The short exits are modeled at $114.627 BRENT and $100.74 OIL on 2026-10-25 at 20:35 MST, after the four earlier replenishments and simultaneous with separate 35U BRENT and 34U OIL LONG Weekend Oil buys; the lot-specific exits override SAR only for the tagged shorts. Those additional buys count toward weekly buy supply, while closing the 10U shorts does not. After the modeled short closures, the buys raise all-core oil long exposure from 65U BRENT + 66U OIL to 100U each (200U combined); this broader exposure is not the scope of the basis-credit table below. This cost-basis calculation remains scoped to the tagged 40U residual plus the two 10U replenishment lots per ticker (60U); it does not fold the separate 35U/34U post-cutoff buys into that prior basis-credit allocation. The entry/exit records remain intact. The target-trim, short-close, and paired cash-out credits are **reclassifications of PnL already recognized in their respective model events**, not additional gains; they must not increase current core PnL a second time. The short exits and paired 50U cash-out are post-cutoff; their realized PnL is folded into this basis-credit reconciliation but remains realized only once in total PnL.

Because model `1U` is a fixed $10,000 entry-notional tranche, not a fixed number of barrels, the effective price basis is computed using the model-implied exposure quantity: `Q = Σ(entry notional ÷ entry price)`. The derived unadjusted effective basis is `combined open long notional ÷ Q`; the cost-credited effective basis is `(combined open long notional − total realized PnL credits) ÷ Q`, where total credits include the trim, separately modeled SHORT-exit PnL, and the specified paired-lot cash-out PnL for the ticker. These are blended, PnL-equivalent prices—not logged entry prices or fills. The simple unit-count average for the modeled open lots is $112.707 for BRENT and $99.817 for OIL; the ratio-equivalent unadjusted blended bases are $112.676581 and $99.804237, respectively. The distinction occurs because the model's 1U is not a fixed barrel quantity. This report applies only to the tagged long lots' effective basis; the SHORT positions are not included as open long exposure, but their explicitly modeled realized PnL is credited against the corresponding ticker's long basis. At any later supplied mark, the basis view must reconcile to standard realized-plus-unrealized PnL without counting either credit a second time. Target-to-rebuy gaps are shown only as quote differences, not dollar-savings estimates.

Dollar amounts and prices are rounded independently for display; cent-rounded components may differ by $0.01 from a cent-rounded total. All basis calculations use unrounded event PnL.

## Portfolio propagation and current cutoff

The CSV maps each ticker's basis credit to all 32 THE ONE MODEL portfolio IDs and their core variants. Each core variant appears 8 times; its position basis is the same for this specific oil cycle because the variant differences do not change these logged long entries or tagged trims. The basis credit is applied once per ticker within each independently funded portfolio; it is not multiplied again on top of the core PnL.

The current EOD model ends on **2026-10-23**. The replenishment entries for BRENT, USOIL (internal ticker OIL) are after that mark cutoff, and there are no post-entry marks with which to update the reported portfolio PnL, deployed-notional ratios, or drawdowns. The replenishments are recorded, and their effective basis is calculated here; they remain outside the cutoff. The two modeled short exits at $114.627 BRENT and $100.74 OIL on 2026-10-25 at 20:35 MST are also post-cutoff. Core and 32-portfolio results through the cutoff include the open shorts' Oct. 23 mark-to-market, but do not include these later replenishment entries or the realized short-exit and paired cash-out PnL. This cost-basis view applies the realized short and cash-out PnL as basis credits only; it does not add them to current core PnL. On a future refresh with subsequent marks, total PnL should be computed as either (a) separate-lot realized plus unrealized PnL, or (b) the equivalent cost-credited basis view—not both added together.
