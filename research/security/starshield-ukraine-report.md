# Starshield & Starlink Slowdown in Ukraine

* **Who capped Ukraine’s Starshield?** “Ultimately POTUS,” Musk said. Ultimately Musk, reports now say.
* **The Pentagon’s stance:** The Pentagon says it gave no order, and Ukraine’s drones were still losing their link above 160 km/h as of 20 October.

---

## Background of the Dispute

* **The initial vacuum:** For a week, no one with a hand on the system said the decision was theirs. 
* **Conflicting claims:** Musk pointed to the US military and the president, while Trump said he did not know who it was. 
* **Operational impact:** Ukrainian officials said the cap has hampered the drones they use to intercept Russian Shaheds and to strike Russian forces in occupied territory. 
* **The speed limit:** Kyiv says the cap remains in place, and at about 160 km/h it makes intercepting jet-powered drones and missiles that fly about twice as fast virtually impossible.

---

## Musk’s Role and Administration Contacts

* **The action:** Musk capped the speeds at which Ukrainian hardware could stay connected to the network, people familiar with the matter told WSJ. 
* **Timeline:** The slowdown, which began on 14 October, left some of Ukraine’s strike and interceptor drones virtually useless, WSJ said.
* **Pre-cap talks:** A source says Musk spoke with administration officials, including Trump, before the cap.
* **Understanding the President's thinking:** Musk held several conversations with administration officials before the order to understand the president’s thinking, CBS reported, citing sources. He dislikes his company being seen as profiting from the war, and it is unclear how his order was carried out, CBS said.
* **WSJ’s sources differ on Trump’s role:** 
  * Some US officials said Musk acted on his own and that Trump did not specifically order any retribution against Ukraine. 
  * One person said Musk spoke with several senior administration officials, Trump among them, before imposing the cap. 
  * Two people said Musk and Trump discussed the decision by phone soon after the slowdown became public.

---

## White House Inquiries and Aftermath

* **Coordination requests:** During the uproar, SpaceX representatives called White House counterparts. They asked for a shared message that the company and the administration had coordinated before Musk’s directive, the people said. 
* **Internal inquiry:** The White House ran an informal inquiry into who ordered the slowdown, WSJ said.

---

## Hegseth and Trump Responses

### Hegseth points to Starlink’s terms of service
* Hegseth said the US had not pulled anything back. 
* He noted that Starlink “has the option” to make decisions under its terms of service.
* From the Pentagon’s view, nothing had changed, CBS reported.

### Trump's Statements
* **16 October:** Speaking to reporters as he left the White House, Trump said he did not think either he or Musk was responsible, The Telegraph reported. 
* **19 October:** He said, “I don’t know who it was, but I’d like to find out.”

---

## Investigations and Contradictory Reports

### Congressional Scrutiny
* Last week, a bipartisan group of senators asked the Pentagon’s inspector general to investigate whether officials “directed” or “authorized” changes to Starlink and Starshield services and intelligence sharing affecting Ukraine.

### Earlier reports pointed at the White House
* **15 October (Reuters):** Reported that the order came from inside the White House. Officials said Pentagon leaders received a message that the Starshield service for Ukraine’s military needed to be slowed, but did not say who made the decision or who carried it out. A senior US official said, “No such request was ever made by the president.”
* **16 October (The Telegraph):** Cited four sources stating that Musk approached Trump with the idea of the speed limits. One of the sources said Trump agreed.

---

## Current Status

### Analysts and Kyiv say the cap is still in place
* **Michael Kofman (Carnegie Endowment):** Stated that Ukrainian strike and interceptor drones remain limited to 160 km/h. He noted that actual connections run below 130 km/h, which still hampers strikes and air defense.
* **20 October (Denys Sienik, Ukraine’s top diplomat in Washington):** Stated that Kyiv was still waiting for answers from the Pentagon and SpaceX, WSJ reported.
