# THE ONE MODEL vs THE WINNING MODEL

> **Historical hypothetical comparison · TradingView-only benchmark through 2026-10-23 · 36 sessions.** This compares the 32-portfolio THE ONE MODEL aggregate with one constituent selected as THE WINNING MODEL: the portfolio with the highest terminal modeled PnL over this same period. Selection is ex post and subject to look-ahead/selection bias.

## Comparison basis

“THE WINNING MODEL” here means **V3A Core + V3A variant-specific 1U/EOD, with the 11U/DAY energy sidecar per active ticker**—portfolio `core_v3a__1u_v3a__with_oil`. It includes core’s integrated 1U/DAY DXY sleeve plus the 10U/DAY DXY add-on (11U/DAY total), just as each of the 32 THE ONE MODEL portfolios does.

The equal-gross comparison scales every daily PnL, notional addition, and open-notional value of this one winning portfolio by **26.598754×**, so its period-end cumulative gross additions match THE ONE MODEL at **$490,880,000.00**. This scales the winner’s unit sizes too: its combined DXY schedule becomes **292.5863U/DAY**, versus the original 11U/DAY. It is a proportional arithmetic scaling of all components, not an assertion that the larger orders could be executed at unchanged prices, spreads, capacity, financing, or risk. The original, unscaled 1× winner is shown alongside it for context; that original portfolio does not have the same total gross additions as the 32-portfolio aggregate.

## Files and interpretation

- Daily comparison series: [`the_one_model_vs_winning_model_v1.csv`](data/the_one_model_vs_winning_model_v1.csv).
- Machine-readable comparison metrics: [`the_one_model_vs_winning_model_v1_summary.json`](data/the_one_model_vs_winning_model_v1_summary.json).
- The equal-gross line is generated by multiplying the winning constituent's daily modeled series by the same constant. Ratios are mathematically invariant to this scaling (aside from cent rounding); dollar drawdowns and notionals scale up.
- The 32-way aggregate assumes independent funding/exposure for every constituent. The one-winner comparison is a single concentrated portfolio. No positions, capital, correlated risk, or margin are netted between the two approaches.
- These are hypothetical paper-model outputs, not brokerage executions or verified account returns. Trading costs, slippage, capacity, financing, margin, taxes, market impact, and operational constraints are not fully modeled. A deployed-notional ratio is **not an account return**.
