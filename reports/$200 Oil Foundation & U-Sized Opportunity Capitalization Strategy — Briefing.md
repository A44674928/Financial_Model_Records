## Opportunity-capitalization purpose and bookkeeping

The U-sized sleeve exists to seek ROI-based income **over the waiting period**—not to replace or liquidate the oil foundation. The intended accounting path is:

> Separately funded U-sized trade → close/realize the result → report realized PnL and ROI on the correct denominator → allocate any realized proceeds only under an explicit cash/reinvestment instruction.

- **Realized PnL only** is eligible to be called income or considered for redeployment. Unrealized gains are not cash income and do not automatically enlarge another sleeve.
- Keep **PnL / gross additions** separate from **PnL / funded cash or maximum gross-open notional**. Gross additions may reflect capital reused after closing positions; they are not automatically new outside cash.
- Keep each book's strategy/model identity, ticker, direction, entry/exit event, user-reported versus modeled status, supplied price/date, and TradingView mark provenance. Do not infer fills or undisclosed trades from a screenshot.
- Use the all-cash/debit convention: fully funded positions, no leverage, borrowing, or financing assumptions. If the U-sized sleeve or any pool allocation changes size, state the explicit scale factor and the new funded base.
- Do not net positions or silently transfer cash across foundation and opportunity sleeves. Any transfer of realized proceeds requires its own dated, auditable rule.
- Keep BRENT and OIL/USOIL as separate instruments and sleeves. Do not convert them to shares or barrels without the relevant contract multiplier; do not double-count DXY or any other sidecar already embedded in a composite.

The objective is ROI-based income, but **neither income nor profitability is assured**. A series of small trades can lose money, incur costs, or remain unrealized. Strategy performance needs a defined benchmark window, explicit denominators, drawdown reporting, and a separate record of realized versus unrealized results.

### 1% Monero end-of-month conversion overlay

The separate modeled payout assumption is **1% of monthly aggregate net realized profit across the included books**, reserved for a dollars-to-Monero conversion at month-end. Realized losses offset gains across books within that month, as directed; this aggregation affects only the payout base and does not net positions or capital. The current overlay scope follows the independently funded books in the $20B pool bridge and does not count alternate sizing views or randomized demo mixes as extra funded books.

This is a **payout/accounting overlay, not a third investment part**. It does not change the two-part architecture, the oil lock, or the separate capital tracking of the included books.

Only realized PnL enters this calculation. If a month’s net realized PnL is positive, 1% is held out from the remaining modeled profit; a non-positive monthly net creates no reserve, and cross-month loss carryforward is not modeled.
